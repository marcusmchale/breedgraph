# BreedGraph Terms of Use: draft

Status: **draft for the University's legal office to confirm.** Not legal advice.
It proposes wording rather than leaving questions open; the notes at the end list what to confirm. Text in [square
brackets] is a fact to fill in before publishing. See `docs/licences.md` for how the Terms and study licences are
accepted, and `docs/read-access.md` for who can read what.

The API serves the published copy, `src/breedgraph/legal/terms_of_use.md` (or the file `TERMS_OF_USE_PATH` names), to
the website and to anyone using the API, and everyone accepts that text before using BreedGraph. To publish a change,
copy these Terms, from their title to the end of "Contact", to that file, and restart the API: the new text has a new
hash, so everyone is asked to accept it again. breedview renders it with a small markdown renderer (headings, bold,
italics, `-` and numbered lists, links, `---` rules), so the Terms have no tables or nested lists.

# BreedGraph Terms of Use

**Last updated: [date]**

## Summary

- BreedGraph is a research data platform for plant breeding, run by the University of Galway.
- You accept these Terms before using BreedGraph, whether through the website or the API.
- Data from a study may come with the study's licence. You accept the licence before you get the data, and you use and pass on the data only as the licence allows.
- Give credit for what you use from BreedGraph, including information anyone can see, such as germplasm and locations.
- Your account, and your acceptance of these Terms and of licences, are yours alone: don't share them.

## Who runs BreedGraph

BreedGraph is run by the University of Galway ("we"). It consists of the BreedGraph API and the BreedView website, at breedgraph.universityofgalway.ie. Contact: [BreedGraph contact address].

## Accepting these Terms

You accept these Terms when you use BreedGraph. The website asks you to accept them before showing anything else, and the API refuses requests until they are accepted. Your acceptance is kept in a cookie in your browser, for 7 days if you are not signed in and for a year if you are, and is asked for again when these Terms change. We keep no record of who accepted them. If you do not accept these Terms, do not use BreedGraph.

## Your account and your acceptances

- Keep your password to yourself, and use only your own account.
- Your acceptance of these Terms and of study licences is personal. Don't pass on, copy or reuse the cookies or tokens that hold them, or use anyone else's.
- Tell us at [BreedGraph contact address] if you think someone else has used your account.

## Data and its licences

- Data in BreedGraph belongs to the teams that hold it, and they decide who can see it.
- A study may name a licence for the use of its data. The licence is set by the data's owners, not by us. Before you get the data of a study with a licence, through the website or the API, you are asked to read and accept it.
- Use the data only as its licence allows. If you pass the data on, pass it on only as the licence allows, together with the licence and the credit it asks for.
- Don't get around the licence step, for example by using the API without accepting the licence, by reusing someone else's acceptance, or by passing data to someone who has not accepted the licence where the licence does not allow it.
- Data without a licence may be used for research, with the credit described below.

## Credit

When you use information from BreedGraph, including information anyone can see, such as germplasm, locations and ontology terms, give appropriate credit: cite BreedGraph, and the study, the people credited and the references that the information names. A licence may ask for more.

## What you add

- Add only data you have the right to add, and attach only licences you have the right to set.
- Don't add personal data beyond what our privacy notice describes.
- You remain responsible for the data you add, and for the release you choose for it.

## Using BreedGraph fairly

- Don't disrupt BreedGraph, try to get past its access controls, or test its security without our agreement.
- Automated use of the API is welcome within these Terms, provided it does not overload BreedGraph.
- We may suspend an account, or remove data, that breaks these Terms.

## Notes for the legal office

1. **How acceptance works.** Everyone ticks a box under the Terms before the website shows anything else, and the API
   refuses every request until the Terms are accepted. Nothing is stored about who accepted: the acceptance is a signed
   token in a cookie in the person's browser, valid for 7 days when signed out and a year when signed in, and only for
   the current text. The same applies to study licences, accepted before their data is shown or analysed. Is this
   adequate to bind users to the Terms and to the licences, including anonymous visitors and users of the API?
2. **Who licenses the data.** The licences are set by the teams that hold the data, often partner organisations, not
   by the University. The Terms say so; please confirm the University's position as the operator.
3. **Credit for information anyone can see.** The Terms ask for credit for public information such as germplasm and
   locations. Is this enforceable, and is the wording right?
4. **Warranty and liability.** The wording in "Our responsibility", and the limit of liability to add.
5. **Law and jurisdiction.** Ireland is proposed. Some users are outside the EEA, and some may be consumers.
6. **Keeping no record of acceptances.** A record for signed-in users could be added later; it would be new personal
   data for the privacy notice. Is it needed?
7. **What users add.** The warranty that users have the right to add data and to set its licence.
8. **Contact and addresses.** The facts in square brackets.
